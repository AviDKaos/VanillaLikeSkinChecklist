# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Journal-style skin menu with one page per item (including the Boat and the Bow), 3D previews, rarity colors and per-item / overall counters.
- Unobtainable skins are hidden by default and excluded from totals, with an in-menu toggle.
- Opens with `K` or by holding Select. Both bindings can be changed in the game's Controls menu.
- Switches between the fish journal and the skin menu with Tab/Select (tap) and K/Select (hold).
- Blocked during cutscenes, credits and loading screens, like the journal.
- One-time tip in the game's tutorial style.
- Translated into all 16 languages the game supports.
